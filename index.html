Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Funnel Rules

The TXD50 index applies the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 methodology across FTSE 350, STOXX Europe 600, and S&P 500 constituents. Step 1 strips out REITs and convertibles. Step 2 requires a minimum 10 consecutive years of dividend distributions. Step 3 enforces the median yield chop (cutting the bottom 50% lowest-yielding companies). Step 4 ranks survivors by cash flow to total debt, return on equity, dividend yield, and five-year dividend growth. Step 5 weights by float market cap with a 4.0% single stock cap and 25.0% sector cap.
